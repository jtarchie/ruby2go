# Decision 349

**Rule:** `String#[]`/`index`/`rindex` inline around out-of-line `(value, found)` helpers; nilable scalars stay `*T`.

**The String#[] family inlines; nilable scalars keep `*T`** (amends 151, 342; 2026-10-23). *See also decision 342.* The recommendation after the Crystal comparison was to represent `Integer?`, `Float?` and `Boolean?` as an unboxed `struct{v T; ok bool}` (Crystal's `Int32 | Nil` is a tagged pair in registers). An allocation profile of the benchmarks said there was little to win: since decision 342, `Hash#[]` and the generic `Array#[]`, `first`, `last`, `index`, `find`, `min`, `max`, `dig`, `bsearch` all inline, so their `&v` stays on the caller's stack. And generic code (`Hash#[]` returns `V?` for every V) could not use a per-type representation anyway. What remained was core methods returning `T?` that are too big to inline, so their result escapes on every call; `String#[](start, count)` was 20% of `collections.rb`'s allocations (`p.name[0, 4] || ""`).
- `String#[](i)`, `#[](start, count)`, `#[](range)`, `#index(str)` and `#rindex(str)` are now a few lines around one out-of-line helper returning `(value, found)` (`rbStrAt`, `rbStrSub`, `rbStrRange`, `rbStrIndex`, `rbStrRindex` in `prelude/go/string.go`), as decision 342 did for `Hash#[]`; each inlines (Go's budget is 80).
- `String#[](i)` no longer converts the whole String to a `[]rune` per call: it walks to the character (`rbRuneOffset`), and checks validity through `rbValidUTF8`'s cache. `String#[](range)` computes the range's start and count itself, without `Range#__slice`'s heap-allocated pair.
- **`rbValidUTF8` allocated on every call.** Its cache store for long subjects (`rbValidCache.Store(&s)`, decision 332) moved the parameter to the heap at entry, so every short String checked (each regexp match on a valid subject, and now `String#[]`) paid a 16-byte allocation that never reached the cache. The store is out of line (`rbValidUTF8Long`). Measured with `GOMAXPROCS=1` (GC on one thread, the stable count): collections 2,016M to 1,969M, strings 561M to 523M.
- Not done: the `struct{v, ok}` representation for nilable scalars. Revisit if a profile shows non-generic `Integer?`/`Float?` escapes; a `T?` of a class (`*CI`, pairing_heap's links, 2.7M allocations a run) is the representation that matters, and it is a decision of its own.
- **Proof:** `string_test.rb` `StringIndexEdgeTest` (negative and out-of-range indexes, multibyte, invalid UTF-8, beginless/endless/exclusive ranges, `index`/`rindex` on multibyte strings, against MRI).
